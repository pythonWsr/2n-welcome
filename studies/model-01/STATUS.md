# Model Study 01 — clay gate NOT PASSED

Independent candidate only. Dark Spatial World source and model are unchanged.

A/B: authored crown and tensioned diagonal; hard diagonal-to-foot junction; load-bearing foot with rear depth growing from 7 to 15 units; separate n portal set five units behind 2.

C: 0.10-unit contour lip, 0.12-unit depth chamfer, flat broad fronts; crease-aware normals. This remains a study, not an accepted sculpture.

D review: inspected two four-view iterations. Front identity and base depth are readable. n's initial roof-like peak was flattened. A nonplanar front experiment produced unwanted triangulation facets and was replaced with one clean pitched plane. Three-quarter still reads too much as a thick typographic plate. The close shot is overly cropped and does not establish a successful fly-by. These findings block acceptance, not just missing device QA.

No E silver pass, no F integration. Next work must improve the massing in three-quarter and n's structural relation, then repeat the clay gate. Do not use lighting to conceal those issues.
